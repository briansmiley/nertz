Nertz! privacy

Nertz is a card game played among friends. You can play without an account, and without telling it anything but the name you want to go by.

If you sign in

With Google, the game keeps the name, email address and profile picture Google has for you, so that it knows you the next time you come. It asks Google for nothing else, and cannot see your password, your mail or anything in your Google account.

With your email, the game keeps your address, and sends you a code to sign in with through Resend, a service for sending email. It sends you nothing else.

Either way, the name you go by is yours to change.

The rounds you play while signed in are kept as your record: when each was played, at which table, how it went, what you scored and who sniped whom. Others who played it signed in have the same round on theirs.

You have a page that shows the name you go by and your record, and anyone looking at it, how they have done against you. Only someone signed in can see it, at its link: the others signed in at a table with you can follow your name there. The link is made at random and says nothing else about you; your email address and your Google picture are not on it.

A cookie in your browser keeps you signed in. Signing out removes it.

While you play

The name you type, your cards and what you write in the chat are shown to the others at your table. Your cards and the chat are forgotten when the table closes. The scores of each round are kept, under the name each was played with; only a round played signed in says which account it was. Voice chat goes between the players' browsers and is not recorded.

Your settings (sound, card backs, the name you last used) are kept in your own browser.

If you send feedback

What you write with the bug button, and the screenshot if you attach one, is kept by the game (as yours, if you are signed in) and goes to the person who runs the game, along with the name you were playing under, the code of your table, and which browser you use and the size of its window. It passes through the services used to keep track of it: Discord, Linear, and Anthropic's Claude, which sorts the reports.

What is not done

No advertising and no tracking. Nothing about you is sold, and apart from the feedback you send, nothing is handed to anyone else. The game runs on a server rented from Fly.io, which is where what it keeps is stored.

Having it removed

Press your name at the foot of the home page and delete your account there, or ask with the bug button in the corner of the game, saying which email address you signed in with. Either way your account is deleted, and your name and account are taken off everything kept: your scores stay as a guest's, and what you sent with the bug button stays with no name to it.
